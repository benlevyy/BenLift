# Evidence Pack — Grounding References for the Judge

The judge uses this list as a starting point for evidence-anchored scoring. It MUST consult external sources (via web_search) before scoring any claim that depends on effect sizes, dose-response, or contraindications — never rely solely on its own priors.

This pack is curated, not exhaustive. Judge may surface additional peer-reviewed or authoritative sources during grading.

## Volume & Hypertrophy

- Schoenfeld BJ, Contreras B, Krieger J, et al. **Resistance Training Volume Enhances Muscle Hypertrophy but Not Strength in Trained Men.** *Med Sci Sports Exerc.* 2019;51(1):94-103. — dose-response: 10+ weekly sets/muscle for hypertrophy.
- Baz-Valle E, Balsalobre-Fernández C, Alix-Fages C, Santos-Concejero J. **A Systematic Review of the Effects of Different Resistance Training Volumes on Muscle Hypertrophy.** *J Hum Kinet.* 2022;81:199-210.
- Schoenfeld BJ. **The mechanisms of muscle hypertrophy and their application to resistance training.** *J Strength Cond Res.* 2010;24(10):2857-72. — mechanical tension + metabolic stress + muscle damage.

## Training to Failure

- Grgic J, Schoenfeld BJ, Orazem J, Sabol F. **Effects of resistance training performed to repetition failure or non-failure on muscular strength and hypertrophy: A systematic review and meta-analysis.** *J Sport Health Sci.* 2022;11(2):202-211. — failure is not required for hypertrophy, and may impair recovery and subsequent volume.
- Helms ER et al. **RIR-based RPE for resistance training.** *Strength Cond J.* 2016. — prescribing RPE 8-10 captures most of the hypertrophy benefit of failure without the fatigue cost.

## Exercise Order

- Nunes JP, Grgic J, Cunha PM, et al. **What influence does resistance exercise order have on muscular strength gains and muscle hypertrophy?** *Eur J Sport Sci.* 2021;21(2):149-157. — exercises performed first receive the greatest strength/hypertrophy gain; prioritize lagging movements in slot 1.

## Recovery Between Sessions

- Bartolomei S, Nigro F, Ruggeri S, et al. **Comparison between bench press throws and ballistic push-ups as measures of upper-body power.** — indirect recovery measure.
- Helms ER, Kwan K, Brown SR, et al. **Training recommendations for natural bodybuilders.** — 48-72h between heavy compound sessions for same muscle group; isolation recovers faster.
- Schoenfeld BJ, Ogborn D, Krieger JW. **Effects of Resistance Training Frequency on Measures of Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.** *Sports Med.* 2016;46(11):1689-1697.

## HRV / Sleep / Readiness

- Flatt AA, Esco MR. **Evaluating individual training adaptation with Smartphone-Derived Heart Rate Variability in a collegiate female soccer team.** *J Strength Cond Res.* 2016;30(2):378-85.
- Knufinke M, Nieuwenhuys A, Geurts SA, et al. **Train hard, sleep well? Perceived training load, sleep quantity and sleep stage distribution in elite level athletes.** *J Sci Med Sport.* 2018;21(4):427-432. — poor sleep acutely reduces force output and training quality.
- Vitale KC, Owens R, Hopkins SR, Malhotra A. **Sleep Hygiene for Optimizing Recovery in Athletes.** *Int J Sports Med.* 2019;40(8):535-543.

## Shoulder Impingement / Rehab

- Kibler WB, Sciascia A, Wilkes T. **Scapular dyskinesis and its relation to shoulder injury.** *J Am Acad Orthop Surg.* 2012;20(6):364-372. — overhead pressing with scapular dyskinesis worsens impingement; favor scapular-plane work.
- Reinold MM, Escamilla RF, Wilk KE. **Current concepts in the scientific and clinical rationale behind exercises for glenohumeral and scapulothoracic musculature.** *J Orthop Sports Phys Ther.* 2009;39(2):105-117. — face pulls, Y-raises, and lateral raises in scapular plane are impingement-tolerant.
- Cools AM, Johansson FR, Borms D, Maenhout A. **Prevention of shoulder injuries in overhead athletes: a science-based approach.** *Braz J Phys Ther.* 2015;19(5):331-339.

## Climbing Load

- Draper N, et al. **Comparative grading scales, statistical analyses, climber descriptors and ability grouping: International Rock Climbing Research Association position statement.** — climbing is primarily a pull + grip + core activity; heavy climbing sessions create meaningful recovery demand on back, biceps, forearms.
- Michailov ML, et al. **Physiological and anthropometric determinants of sport climbing performance.** — grip endurance and forearm fatigue specific to bouldering.

## Authoritative Bodies

- **ACSM Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription, 11th Ed.**
- **NSCA Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning, 4th Ed.**
- **Renaissance Periodization** — practical volume landmarks (MEV/MAV/MRV) by muscle group, Mike Israetel.
- **Stronger by Science** — Greg Nuckols' evidence reviews.

---

The judge is expected to cite 1-3 of these (or additional peer-reviewed sources it retrieves via web_search) when a scored dimension depends on contested claims or effect sizes.
